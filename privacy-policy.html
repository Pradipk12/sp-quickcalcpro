Privacy Policy

SP QuickCalc — Pro Suite

SP QuickCalc is a calculator utility (standard, scientific, EMI/finance, date, and unit conversion tools). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

No data collection

SP QuickCalc does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All calculations, inputs (loan amounts, dates, converted values), and your calculation history run entirely on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Numbers you type into the calculator, EMI, date, or converter tools
  • Your calculation history (the "tape")
  • Your light/dark theme preference

This information is stored locally in your browser or device storage and is never sent to any server. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Network access

The app loads two fonts and a math library from public content-delivery networks (Google Fonts and cdnjs) the first time it runs, purely to render the interface and perform calculations. These requests do not include any personal or usage data — they're the same as loading any public web font or script.

Permissions

SP QuickCalc does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, storage, or any other device permission beyond standard internet access for the resources above.

Third parties

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's privacy

Since no personal data is collected from anyone, the app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address listed on the app's Google Play Store listing.
